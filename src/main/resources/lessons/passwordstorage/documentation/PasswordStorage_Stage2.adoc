== Stage 2: a unique salt

The developers now generate a random salt for each account and store:

[source,text]
----
SHA-256(UTF-8(password) || saltBytes)
----

The salt appears beside the hash in the database. That is expected. A salt is not a secret.

Alice and Bob still use the same password, but their records use different salts. Compare their hashes. Then use Maria's public salt and the supplied wordlist to recover her password.

A unique salt prevents direct hash reuse across accounts and defeats precomputed tables that omit the salt. It does not slow down SHA-256 or make a weak password strong.
